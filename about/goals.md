### Friday Night Dungeon looks to accomplish the following:
* Fun, speedy combat.
* Roleplay focus.
* Simple.

### Problem Solving
Most modern TTRPGs focus on problem solving with your character sheet.  There is a trap?  Check which character has the biggest number and then have him roll dice.  Do math.  Compare the result to the rules and you've got your outcome.

This is okay, but not what excites me.  I would rather solve problems with my decision making.  My character sheet lets me know what my character is good at, but what really drives the story are my choices and creativity.
