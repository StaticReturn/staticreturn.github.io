# Berzerker
A person who fights in a trance-like fury.

*  <i class="fa-solid fa-fingerprint" aria-hidden="true"></i>: While being reckless in combat gain 1 point of damage reduction.
*  <i class="fa-solid fa-hand-fist" aria-hidden="true"></i>: When melee attacking in a reckless manner add 1 damage.
*  <i class="fa-solid fa-bullhorn" aria-hidden="true"></i>: After killing an opponent grant taunt to all short range opponents.
*  <i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved" aria-hidden="true"></i>: You are immune to any mental condition.
