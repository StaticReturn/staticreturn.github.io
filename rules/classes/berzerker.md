# Berzerker
A person who fights in a trance-like fury.

*  :fingerprint: &nbsp;**Rage**: &nbsp;While being reckless in combat gain 1 point of damage reduction.
*  :swords: &nbsp;**Attack**: &nbsp;When melee attacking in a reckless manner add 1 damage.
*  :shield: &nbsp;**Defense**: &nbsp;You are immune to any mental condition.
*  :superpowers: &nbsp;**Dangerous**: &nbsp;After killing an opponent grant taunt to all short range opponents.
