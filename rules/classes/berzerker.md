# Berzerker
A person who fights in a trance-like fury.

*  :fingerprint: Behavior: While being reckless in combat gain 1 point of damage reduction.
*  :swords: Attack: When melee attacking in a reckless manner add 1 damage.
*  :shield: Defense: You are immune to any mental condition.
*  :bullhorn: Skill: After killing an opponent grant taunt to all short range opponents.
