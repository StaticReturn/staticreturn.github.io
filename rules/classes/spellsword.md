# Spellsword
A person who mixes melee with a tactical use of close-combat spells.

* :fingerprint: **Behavior**: If not damaged for a round and in melee combat, mark a target for 1 turn.  All allies deal 1 extra damage to that target.
* :swords: **Magic Bolt**: Perform a short range attack that ignores 1 point of damage reduction.
* :shield: **Magic Shield**: Reduce the damage to any target within long range by 1.
* :superpowers: **Enchanted Movement**: Take no fall damage.
