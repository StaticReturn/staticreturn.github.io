# Brawler
A person who fights effectively with his bare fists.

* :fingerprint: **Champion Fighter**: When using anatomy or leverage against an opponent your unarmed attacks treat the opponent as having one less defense.
* :swords: **Attack**: Ignore bludgeoning resistance.
* :shield: **Defense**: Gain bludgeoning resistance.
* :superpowers: **Slippery**: You cannot be grappled.
