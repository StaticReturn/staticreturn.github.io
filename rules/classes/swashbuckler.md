# Swashbuckler
A person who engages in daring adventures with bravado or flamboyance.

* :fingerprint: **Behavior**: Gain ten feet of free movement after every attack.
* :swords: **Attack**: You may attack an opponent that has left your melee range.
* :shield: **Defense**: You may ignore any effect that forces you to move.
* :superpowers: **Skill**: You do not need to make a challenge roll while maneuvering for a difficulty level of moderate or easy.
