# Duelist
A person who excels at one-on-one combat.

* :fingerprint: **Maneuver**: When you have isolated an opponent from his allies he now deals one less damage.
* :swords: **Riposte**: In a one-on-one fight if an opponent tries to attack you, you may parry and riposte if your opponent does not spend 1 action to resist.
* :shield: **Parry**: In a one-on-one fight you may parry one melee attack.
* :superpowers: **Disarm**: Disarm an opponent that has just attacked you if your opponent does not spend 1 action to resist.
