# Fighter
A person who is familiar with the tools of combat and can extract the most out of them.

* :fingerprint: **Tactics**: If you have not used the same weapon to deal damage since last round deal one more point of damage.
* :swords: **Attack**: Deal two less your normal damage to up to three opponents within melee range.
* :shield: **Defense**: You may block 1 ranged attack targeting you.
* :superpowers: **Battle Worn**: You may use two tags of a weapon at once.
