# Swashbuckler
A person who engages in daring adventures with bravado or flamboyance.

*  :fingerprint: &nbsp;**Behavior**: &nbsp;Gain ten feet of free movement after every attack.
*  :swords: &nbsp;**Attack**: &nbsp;You may attack an opponent that has left your melee range.
*  :shield: &nbsp;**Defense**: &nbsp;You may ignore any effect that forces you to move.
*  :superpowers: &nbsp;**Skill**: &nbsp;You do not need to make a challenge roll while maneuvering for a difficulty level of moderate or easy.
