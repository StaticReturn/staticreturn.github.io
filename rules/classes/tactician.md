# Tactician
All of your abilities depend on watching the battlefield.  They may only be performed on targets that you have not lost sight of since your last turn.

* :fingerprint: **A Call of Warning**: Shout at friend or foe. Add or subtract 10 movement to the source of your speech.
* :swords: **Attack**: You grant an ally 1 additional damage.
* :shield: **Defense**: Your ally takes 1 less point of damage.
* :superpowers: **Stall**: A monster you haven't targeted before that is level 2 or lower loses his turn.
