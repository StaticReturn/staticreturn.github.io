# Assassin
Strike from the shadows.

*  :fingerprint: &nbsp;**One With the Shadows**: &nbsp;If you started your turn with cover you gain 1 damage to all of your attacks.
*  :swords: &nbsp;**Attack**: &nbsp;If you strike an opponent who hasn't seen you in combat, perform a critical hit.
*  :shield: &nbsp;**Defense**: &nbsp;If you have partial cover treat it as full cover.
*  :superpowers: &nbsp;**Move With Ease**: &nbsp;Treat all difficult terrain and 10 foot obsticles as normal terrain.
