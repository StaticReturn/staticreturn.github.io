# Assassin
Strike from the shadows.

* :fingerprint: **One With the Shadows**: If you started your turn with cover you gain 1 damage to all of your attacks.
* :swords: **Attack**: If you strike an opponent who hasn't seen you in combat, perform a critical hit.
* :shield: **Defense**: If you have partial cover treat it as full cover.
* :superpowers: **Move With Ease**: Treat all difficult terrain and 10 foot obstacles as normal terrain.
