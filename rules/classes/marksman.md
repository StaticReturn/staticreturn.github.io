# Marksman
A person who is exceptionally accurate and skilled with ranged weapons.

* :fingerprint: **Great Shot**: When attempting a called shot deal one more point of damage or reduce the called shot to a normal attack.
* :swords: **Attack**: You may split your ranged weapon damage among any number of opponents.
* :shield: **Defense**: While wearing light or no armor and no enemy is within melee range, gain resistance to ranged attacks.
* :superpowers: **Aim**: Call out a target during your turn to deal an additional point of damage to that target next turn.
