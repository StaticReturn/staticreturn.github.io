# Wizard
Spells, but at a price.  Wizards all have vulnerability to physical damage.  Wizards all start at 20 mana.

### Spells

| Cost  | Name       | Description |
| ----- | ----------- | ----------- |
| **1** | Magic Missile |  Perform a long range attack that is always a called shot. |
| **2x** | Speed |  You, and 1 target for each 2 mana spent, get double movement until your next turns. |
| **2** | Circle of Protection |  All allies in a 10 foot radius of the target point gain 1 armor.  5 mana per turn to maintain.  |
| **5** | Slow Ground |  Create a patch of ground that halves the movement of all inside it.  Up to 100 sq ft.  5 mana per turn to maintain. |
| **6** | Fire Ball |  Cause a blast of fire to damage everything within thirty foot radius of a target.  The damage is equal to half the amount of mana you use to cast this spell.  |
| **?** | Explosive Trap |  Choose a five foot square.  As a free action you may detonate the square to cause damage equal to the mana spent to cast this spell. |
| **?** | Seal |  Create an impenetrable barrier no more than 100 sq ft.  Its HP is equal to the initial amount of mana spent to cast this spell.  5 mana per turn to maintain. |
